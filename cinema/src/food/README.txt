CANTEEN PHOTOS  (real food photos for Twin Cinema’s Canteen)
=========================================================
Put one photo per item in THIS folder, named exactly as below.
  - JPG / PNG / WEBP, landscape 4:3, about 800x600, under 150 KB each (they are bundled into the app)
  - Use real, appetising photos (Pexels / Unsplash — check each licence).

HOW IT WORKS
  - As soon as ANY photo is in this folder, the canteen lists ONLY the items that have a photo.
  - With no photos at all it shows the whole menu on plum icon tiles (setup mode).
  - So add the best photos you have; items without a photo simply do not appear.
