Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Twin Hearts ("we", "the app") is a private space for couples. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you sign up or sign in, we collect your name, email address, phone number, gender, and an encrypted password and vault PIN. If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your Google name, email address and profile picture from Google. We do not receive your Google password.

We also store the content you add to the app, such as messages, photos and notes shared with your partner.

How we use it

We use your information only to create and secure your account, connect you with your partner, and run the features of the app. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers.

Storage and security

Your data is stored with our database provider (Supabase). Passwords and PINs are stored in hashed form. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Your choices

You can ask us to view, correct or delete your account and all of your data at any time by emailing us at the address below. We will act on your request promptly.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.
